SHANTI VIKASA — WINDOWS UPDATE 1.0.1

1. In your existing app, click Back up data and keep the .sqlite backup.
2. Close the app. Open ShantiVikasa-Setup-1.0.1.exe and install it.
3. Open Shanti Vikasa from the same desktop shortcut.

Your current inventory and receipts stay at:
  %APPDATA%\ShantiVikasa\register.sqlite
The installation stays at:
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\ShantiVikasa
Do not delete or replace your database folder.

PRODUCT PHOTOS
Inventory > Edit (pencil) > Choose photo / Change photo.
Choose JPG, PNG, or WebP, check the preview, then Save product.
Remove photo clears the photo when you save. New products also have a photo picker.
Original catalog pictures are kept unless you change them.

REMOVE AN INVENTORY ITEM
Inventory > trash icon > Remove item.
The item disappears from the register and connected inventory. Past receipts are
kept. Its old product code remains reserved.

CONNECT THE WEBSITE
The website must first be deployed with its online database and shop password.
Open Connect website, enter your Vercel website address, and sign in.
Your first connection uploads this PC's current inventory, photos and receipts
into the empty online register. No current-PC backup needs to be sent to ChatGPT.

Changes sync on startup, after edits, and about every 15 seconds while connected.
The Windows app continues working offline and shows how many changes are waiting.
Use Sync now whenever you want to check. Sign in again if the session expires.
The website requires internet. This does not connect to the separate Shopify store.

If both devices edit the same field, syncing pauses and asks which edit to keep.
Past receipts are never deleted. Offline stock conflicts must be corrected online
before the affected sale can finish syncing; the local receipt remains safe.

BACKUPS
Back up data includes inventory, receipts, and custom photos. Save a copy outside
this computer. Automatic daily copies are also kept in the backups folder.
Restore makes a safety copy and disconnects this computer from online syncing.
Online data is kept. Reconnect deliberately after reviewing which data you need.

CHECK ON YOUR SHOP PC
Confirm the existing product and sales totals after installing. Test your USB
scanner, camera and receipt printer. Windows installation and actual hardware
have not been run in this Linux build environment. The installer is unsigned.

Card checkout records payment from an external card terminal; it does not charge
cards. No stock quantities are invented for uncounted catalog products.
